HIGHLY EXPERIMENTAL NIGHTLY BUILD

This is not a release of Pelton. It is an automated nightly build, produced
from the development branch without review, testing or approval of any kind.
It exists so that people who want to help test unfinished work can do so.

WHAT THIS MEANS

  * It is expected to be broken. Features may be half-finished, newly added
    code may never have been run by anyone before you, and the build may fail
    to start at all.

  * It can lose or damage email. It may fail to send, send the wrong thing,
    delete messages on your server, or corrupt its local cache of your mail.
    Deletions on an email server can be permanent and cannot be undone by us.

  * DO NOT USE IT WITH YOUR REAL INBOX. Use a test account whose contents you
    can afford to lose entirely.

  * It has no support. There is no guarantee that a nightly will be fixed,
    replaced, kept available, or ever built again.

A nightly build stores its accounts, mail and settings in its own separate
location and does not read or write the data of a normally installed Pelton.
Installing it will not disturb an existing Pelton installation, and the two
can be installed side by side.
